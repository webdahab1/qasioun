Qasioun Digital Experience

Premium Website Concept & Interactive 3D Hero

⸻

1. Project Overview

Create a premium digital experience for Qasioun Restaurant in Irbid, Jordan.

The goal is not to build a traditional restaurant website.

The goal is to transform Qasioun’s online presence from a simple social media presence into a complete hospitality experience that represents the restaurant as a destination.

Qasioun is not only about food. It represents:

* Restaurant experience
* Family atmosphere
* Outdoor seating
* Zoo garden experience
* Events and celebration halls
* Traditional Levantine hospitality

⸻

2. Creative Vision

Main Concept:

“A journey through Qasioun”

The visitor should feel like they are entering the restaurant digitally before visiting physically.

The website should communicate:

* Warm hospitality
* Syrian/Damascene heritage
* Family experiences
* Premium dining
* Memorable occasions

The experience should feel closer to a luxury hotel, resort, or destination website rather than a normal restaurant website.

⸻

3. Brand Direction

Brand Personality:

* Elegant
* Traditional
* Warm
* Family-oriented
* Premium
* Welcoming

Inspiration:

Damascene heritage combined with Irbid hospitality.

Visual inspiration:

* Traditional stone architecture
* Arabic patterns
* Wooden details
* Warm lighting
* Green spaces
* Water elements
* Authentic dining atmosphere

⸻

4. Website Main Goal

The website should help visitors:

1. Discover the Qasioun experience
2. Explore different areas of the restaurant
3. View the menu easily
4. Book visits and events
5. Understand that Qasioun is more than a restaurant

⸻

5. Hero Section Concept

The hero should be the strongest visual element.

Preferred direction:

Interactive 3D Experience

Using Spline or similar web 3D technology.

The scene should represent a digital journey inside Qasioun.

⸻

Hero Scenario:

Scene 1:

The visitor starts outside a beautiful traditional entrance.

Elements:

* Stone entrance
* Qasioun logo integration
* Warm lights
* Plants
* Traditional details

Camera slowly moves forward.

⸻

Scene 2:

The camera enters the main courtyard.

Elements:

* Fountain
* Outdoor seating
* Traditional textures
* Trees and greenery
* Lantern lighting
* Relaxing family atmosphere

⸻

Scene 3:

The experience reveals different zones:

Restaurant Area

Shows:

* Dining atmosphere
* Tables
* Food experience

Family Zoo Garden

Shows:

* Green spaces

Events & Halls

Shows:

* Celebration spaces

⸻

6. Interactive Elements

Possible interactions:

* Explore restaurant
* View outdoor seating
* Discover family area
* Explore halls
* Open menu
* Book reservation

Avoid making it feel like a game.

7. Visual Style

Preferred style:

Semi-realistic premium 3D

Not:

* Cartoon
* Game style
* Overly futuristic

The feeling should be:

* Luxury hospitality
* Modern website experience
* Authentic cultural identity

⸻

8. Technical Requirements

Technology direction:

* Spline 3D for hero experience
* Web optimized assets
* Fast loading

* Smooth animations
